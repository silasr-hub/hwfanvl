Publishing & store optimization

Hand-crafted store pages that earn the tap. We write the copy, art-direct the screenshots, pick the right category and keywords, and run continuous ASO experiments on title, subtitle, and creative.

Live-ops & content updates

A shipped app is a living product. We run fortnightly events, holiday drops, balance patches, and server-configurable difficulty — plus the analytics review that decides what we ship next.

Privacy & compliance

07

User acquisition consulting

We don't buy media on your behalf, but we'll set up the campaigns, build the creatives, and tune the bids so your UA team — or ours — gets profitable cohorts from day one.

  • Cohort LTV modeling & payback windows
  • Apple Search Ads & UAC best practices
08

Localization & internationalization

Translate once, tune for every market. We coordinate native linguists, adapt UI strings, rework screenshots, and re-rank keywords per locale — so an app that wins in Brazil doesn't lose in Japan.

Ad mediation & monetization

Every impression, fairly auctioned.

We don't pick a single ad partner and hope. We run a layered mediation stack — with AdMob as the backbone, TopOn and TradPlus as the optimization layer, and a deep bench of premium networks as bid sources — then tune the waterfalls per region, per device, and per session.

01

Splash ad

Cold open

A 3–5 second brand moment at launch, served before the first interactive frame. We cap frequency per session and skip the splash if the user has already seen it inside the same hour — so it never gets in the way of an engaged user.

  • Best for: Casual games with a 3-tap return
  • eCPM tier: High on cold opens, low repeat
  • UX rule: Always skippable after 3 seconds
02

Rewarded video

Opt-in · best LTV

The user picks to watch a 15–30 second video in exchange for a meaningful in-app reward — extra lives, a power-up, a streak saver, an unlock. We treat rewarded as a feature, not an interruption, and never auto-play it.

  • Best for: Game progression, utility unlocks
  • eCPM tier: Highest CPM across all formats
  • UX rule: Opt-in only, never on a timer
03

Interstitial

Natural break

A full-screen ad at a moment that already feels like a pause — between levels, after a task, before a save point. We cap to one interstitial per session-per-chapter and respect a 60-second cooldown after the last one.

  • Best for: Between-level, post-task
  • eCPM tier: Mid-high, scales with fill
  • UX rule: Frequency cap & session cap enforced
04

Banner

Always-on, low-touch

A small, quiet strip at the bottom of a screen — ideal for utility apps where the user is already staying. We use adaptive banners, auto-refresh after 30 seconds, and never block a primary action with a sticky placement.

  • Best for: Utility screens, long sessions
  • eCPM tier: Steady baseline, low variance
  • UX rule: Never above the fold, never on inputs
Our philosophy

User experience first.
Data a close second.

We will never trade a 0.4-point retention drop for a 6% eCPM lift. The studio's job is to keep people opening the app tomorrow — and the mediation layer's job is to make every impression worth the moment. The two are not in tension if you instrument them honestly.

UX-first frequency caps

Hard limits per session, per day, per genre — tuned per app, not inherited from a template.

eCPM waterfall optimization

Per-region, per-device, per-placement bidding — refreshed weekly from the last 30 days of fill data.

A/B-tested ad placements

Every change goes through a 7-day split test with a primary KPI and a guardrail metric before it ships to 100%.

Hybrid monetization

Ads plus a soft in-app store for power users, with rewarded video as the on-ramp — never pay-to-win.

How we ship

Six steps. One release train.

The same playbook for a 6-week utility app, a 12-week casual game, and a 4-week mediation re-stack. Each phase has a clear owner, a clear exit, and a clear handoff to the next.

01

Discovery

A 2-week sprint to align on the player, the problem, the success metric, and the smallest version that can credibly prove the idea.

02

Design

Wireframes, a paper prototype, a brand pass, and a clickable build — all reviewed weekly, all tested with five users before a single line of code lands in the repo.

03

Build

Two-week sprints, every Friday a TestFlight or internal-track build, every Friday a written demo. No silent months, no last-minute reveals.

04

Test

Internal QA, a paid external testing pool, an accessibility pass, a low-end device pass, and a final store-policy review before we even think about pressing submit.

05

Launch

Staged rollout, ASO, press kit, in-app onboarding tuned for week-one cohorts, and a launch-day war-room for the first 48 hours of crashes and reviews.

06

Iterate

A quarterly roadmap, a fortnightly event calendar, and a Monday-morning dashboard review that decides what the team ships next week.
